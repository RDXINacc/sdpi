8. Perform Velocity Analysis and NMO Correction:

9. DMO:
sed 's/nmo/dmo/g; s/\\//g' vpick.txt > dmo.txt
susort offset cdp < nmo.su | sudmofk par=dmo.txt cdpmin=101 cdpmax=636 dxcdp=1 noffmix=9 > dmo.su
susort cdp offset < dmo.su | sustack key=cdp normpow=1 > dmostack.su
suximage < dmostack.su perc=98 title="DMO Stack" &




10. Stacking:
ls -lh velfiltcdpsmuterstat.su vpick1.txt sourcestatic1.dat recstatic1.dat
sunmo < velfiltcdpsmuterstat.su par=vpick.txt | sushw key=tstat a=0 | sushw key=sstat a=0 | sushw key=gstat a=0 | sustatic hdrs=3 sou_file=sourcestatic1.dat rec_file=recstatic1.dat ns=167 nr=668 no=96 | sustack key=cdp normpow=1 > rstack3.su
suximage < rstack3.su perc=90 title="Final Stacked Section (rstack3.su)" &
